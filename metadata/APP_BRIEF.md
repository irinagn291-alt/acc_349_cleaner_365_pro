# Cleaner 365

## What it is

Cleaner 365 helps people review photos and videos, collect items for deletion, and keep private photo copies in a vault. It also offers password and email security tools for people who want to manage sign-ins and check for exposed credentials.

## Launch and onboarding

The app first shows a loading screen while it starts. The native home screen is titled “Today”; there is no separate onboarding sequence.

Before photo access is granted, the main card says “First round” and “Pick a month and review your first 20 photos. Swipe left to queue a photo or right to keep it.”

1. Tap “Open my photos” to request photo access, or “Try three practice cards” to try a round without accessing Photos.
2. If photo access has been denied or restricted, “Open Settings” opens the app’s settings instead.
3. Once photos are available, the card shows a month and progress, with “Start round”, “Resume round”, or “Month results” according to its progress.

If access is granted but there are no photos, the card says “No photos available yet” and “Add pictures to Photos or try the practice round.”

## Screens

### Today

“Today” shows the current season, a suggested month and its review progress. “Start round”, “Resume round”, or “Month results” opens that month’s round.

- “Reminders”, the bell control, opens reminder settings.
- “Vault” opens the private photo vault.
- “Cleanup” opens four photo and video collections. Its subtitle is “Duplicates, captures and large videos”.
- “Security” opens the security tools. Its subtitle is “Passwords, aliases and breach checks”.
- “Months” shows a year selector and month tiles with reviewed and total counts. Select a year and tap a month with photos to open its round.
- “Choose more photos” appears with limited photo access and opens the system photo selection.
- “Review bin”, subtitled “Check before deleting”, opens the bin and shows its item count.
- “Try three practice cards” opens “Practice”.

### Photo round

The screen title is the selected month. It shows a date, a photo or Live Photo, progress through the current round, and a “Favorite” marker where applicable.

- Swipe left or tap “To bin” to queue the photo; swipe right or tap “Keep” to keep it in Photos.
- Tap the photo to open “Preview”. “Done” closes the preview. Photos can be zoomed; videos use playback controls where shown.
- “Undo swipe” reverses the most recent choice made in the current round.
- “Review bin” opens the bin.
- “Round options” contains “Review the rest of this month”, which starts a round containing the remaining photos.
- At completion, “Round complete” shows “Kept” and “In bin” counts. “Review bin” opens the bin, “Next 20 photos” starts another batch when photos remain, and “Back to home” returns home.
- If access changes, the screen says “Photo access changed” and offers “Back to home”.

### Practice

“Practice” presents three sample cards: “The stadium”, “The bend”, and “Finish line”. It says “Swipe left to queue. Swipe right to keep.”

“To bin” and “Keep” make the same choices as swiping. “Undo swipe” reverses a choice. After all three cards, “Round complete” shows “Kept” and “In bin” counts, and “Back to home” returns home. Practice does not act on the photo library.

### Cleanup and collections

“Cleanup” says “Four collections. One review bin.” and “Nothing leaves Photos until you confirm.” Without photo access, “Choose photos” requests access, or “Open Settings” opens settings.

The collection links show counts and open:

- “Exact duplicates” — “Identical files, including Live Photo motion”.
- “Screenshots” — “Captures from your photo library”.
- “Screen recordings” — “Videos recorded from your screen”.
- “Large videos” — “100 MB or larger · biggest first”.

For limited access, the screen says “Showing the items you shared with Cleaner 365.” While originals are checked, it says “Checking originals…”. If items could not be fully checked, “Scan with iCloud downloads” retries with iCloud originals. “Review bin” opens the bin.

Each collection displays matching items with dates and relevant details. Tap a thumbnail, labelled “Preview item”, to open “Preview”; “Done” closes it. Use “Select item” and “Deselect item” to change the selection. “Select extra copies” selects eligible duplicate copies; “Select all available” selects eligible items in the other collections. “Deselect all” clears the selection. “Queue selection” adds selected items to the bin, and “Review bin” opens it.

“Exact duplicates” says “Keep at least one copy in every set. Favorites stay in Photos.” Items already queued show “In review bin”; favorites show “Favorite · protected”.

### Review bin

“Review bin” holds queued items until the person decides what to do. It says “Select items to keep or delete. Tap a thumbnail for a closer look.”

- Tap “Preview item” to inspect an item; “Done” closes its preview.
- “Select item” and “Deselect item” change individual selections. “Select all” and “Deselect all” change the whole selection.
- “Keep” returns selected items to Photos without deleting them. “Keep everything in Photos” clears the entire bin.
- “Delete” asks Photos to delete the selected items. The screen warns: “With iCloud Photos, deletion syncs across your devices. You can usually recover items from Recently Deleted for 30 days.”
- Where available, the selected items’ “Original size” is displayed.

An empty bin says “Your bin is empty” and “Items queued from a round or a cleanup collection stay in Photos until you confirm deletion.” “Done” returns to the previous screen.

### Vault

“Vault” opens to “Private vault” with “Open your photos and Live Photos with Face ID.” “Unlock with Face ID” authenticates and opens “My collection”. The screen warns: “This vault lives on this iPhone. Before changing devices, removing the app, or changing Face ID, restore the photos you want to keep.”

In “My collection”:

- “Add from Photos” opens “Add to vault”, requesting photo access if needed. Select photos or Live Photos, then tap “Add” with the selected count; “Cancel” closes the picker.
- “Manage photo access” changes the shared selection when access is limited.
- After copies are verified, “Verified copies” shows a count. “Remove originals from Photos” requests deletion of eligible originals; “Keep originals” dismisses that choice.
- Tap a “Photo” or “Live Photo” tile to see it. “All photos” returns to the collection. “Restore to Photos” saves a copy to Photos while keeping the vault copy. An eligible original can also be removed with “Remove original from Photos”.
- “Lock vault” locks the collection. It also locks when leaving the screen or sending the app to the background.

The empty collection says “Add your first photos. After verification, you can remove their originals from Photos.” The removal notice says “With iCloud Photos, removal syncs across your devices. Originals stay in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days.”

### Security

“Security” says “Protect your sign-ins and check for exposure.” It links to four tools:

- “Password generator” — “Build a random password on this iPhone”. It displays a generated password. “Length”, “Uppercase letters”, “Numbers”, and “Symbols” change its settings and regenerate it; lowercase letters are always included. “New password” generates another. “Copy password” copies it and changes to “Copied for 1 minute”.
- “Email aliases” — “Forward mail to your own inbox”. The setup explains that SimpleLogin forwards incoming mail to a verified mailbox. “Open SimpleLogin” opens its setup page. Enter a “Personal API key” and tap “Connect SimpleLogin”. Once connected, “Create forwarding alias” creates an address; “Refresh” reloads the list; “Load more aliases” appears when more may be available. Each alias shows “Active” or “Disabled”, its forwarding inbox, and “Copy alias”, which changes to “Copied for 1 minute”. “Manage mailboxes in SimpleLogin” opens mailbox management, and “Disconnect this iPhone” removes the connection from this device.
- “Email breach check” — “Find breaches linked to your address”. Enter an address in “name@example.com” and tap “Check email”. The screen says the address is sent to Have I Been Pwned and its results open in a browser sheet.
- “Password breach check” — “Check for an exposed password”. Enter a password in “Enter a password” and tap “Check password”. The screen says “Only the first five characters of its hash leave this iPhone. The full match is checked locally.” Results show “No match found” or “Password exposed”, with the number of occurrences when exposed. “Data from Have I Been Pwned” opens the data source.

### Reminders

“Reminders” has a “Monthly review” toggle. It schedules one reminder on the first of each month at 7 PM local time, with no sound or badge. The notification reads “Your monthly photo round” and “Pick up where you left off in Cleaner 365.” Tapping it opens the suggested photo round when one is available.

If notifications are denied, “Notification settings” opens system settings.

### Widget and Apple Watch

The “Season progress” widget shows photos reviewed and progress; the medium widget also shows the review queue count. Before progress is available it says “Your first round” and “Open Cleaner 365”. Tapping it opens the suggested round in the iPhone app.

The Apple Watch companion shows “Photos reviewed”, progress, “In review bin”, and the last update time. Before receiving a summary it says “Your first round” and “Open Cleaner 365 on your iPhone to see your review progress.” “Refresh” requests an update from the iPhone.

## Features

- Monthly photo review in rounds of 20, with “To bin”, “Keep”, and “Undo swipe”.
- “Review the rest of this month” and month-by-month progress.
- “Practice” with three sample cards.
- “Exact duplicates”, “Screenshots”, “Screen recordings”, and “Large videos” collections.
- A “Review bin” for previewing, keeping, or confirming deletion of queued items.
- A Face ID-protected “Vault” for photos and Live Photos, with verified copies and restoration to Photos.
- “Password generator”, “Email aliases”, “Email breach check”, and “Password breach check”.
- “Monthly review” reminders, the “Season progress” widget, and an Apple Watch progress companion.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- Months without photos are disabled. Add or share photos for that month to open it. With no accessible photos, use “Try three practice cards” or add pictures to Photos.
- A round stops at 20 photos by default. After “Round complete”, tap “Next 20 photos”, or use “Review the rest of this month” from “Round options”.
- Protected photos cannot be queued in some circumstances. The alert may say “This photo is protected. You can keep it in Photos.” Choose “Keep”.
- Duplicate selection preserves a copy. “One matching copy must stay in Photos.” or “Keep at least one copy of this file in Photos.” means another copy must remain unselected. Favorites are protected.
- “Queue selection”, “Keep”, and “Delete” are disabled until an eligible item is selected. The bin starts with “Your bin is empty”; queue items from a round or collection first.
- Cleanup may show “No exact duplicates found”, “No screenshots found”, “No screen recordings found”, or “No large videos found”. If originals could not be checked, try “Scan with iCloud downloads”.
- “Unlock with Face ID” requires Face ID to be set up. If unavailable, the notice says “Set up Face ID on this iPhone to unlock your vault.” The vault locks when its screen is left or the app backgrounds.
- “Add” in the vault picker is disabled until at least one photo is selected. Originals remain in Photos after import unless their removal is separately confirmed. Favorites cannot be removed through the vault.
- “Connect SimpleLogin” requires a key. “Create forwarding alias” waits for the alias list to refresh; after a creation error, use “Refresh” before trying again. The empty list says “Your first alias will appear here.”
- “Check email” requires a valid email address. “Check password” requires a password and is disabled while checking. Sensitive security fields and the generated password are cleared when the app backgrounds.
- “Monthly review” may require notification permission. If denied, use “Notification settings”; the screen can say “Allow notifications in Settings to schedule your reminder.”
- The watch can say “Open Cleaner 365 on your iPhone to update.” or “Your iPhone isn't available right now.” Open the iPhone app and use “Refresh”.

## Starter content and resume

“Practice” contains the three sample cards “The stadium”, “The bend”, and “Finish line”. Photo review decisions and the bin are retained, and “Resume round” returns to unfinished review work.

## Permissions

- **Photos:** Requested through “Open my photos”, “Choose photos”, or “Add from Photos”. Usage description: “Review photos and videos. You choose which originals to delete from Photos.”
- **Add to Photos:** Requested when restoring a vault item to Photos. Usage description: “Restore selected photos and Live Photos from your vault.”
- **Face ID:** Requested when unlocking the vault. Usage description: “Use Face ID to open your private photo vault.”
- **Notifications:** Requested when enabling “Monthly review”. There is no separate usage-description string.

## Absent

The native app has no app account or login, in-app purchase, ads, account deletion flow, or App Tracking Transparency prompt. The alias feature connects to a separate SimpleLogin account.

User-generated content is present in the form of photos and Live Photos selected for review or the vault. The visible native screens do not establish whether analytics are absent.

## Data and support

Review decisions and vault copies are kept on the device. The watch and widget receive review progress; iCloud Photos deletion can sync across devices. Security checks send the email address, or a partial password hash, to Have I Been Pwned; alias operations use SimpleLogin.

There is no on-screen Contact or Support control in the native screens.

## Scanning and health

None. The app does not scan barcodes or QR codes and does not show health, medical, or product-health information.

## Platform

The native interface uses English and formats displayed dates and numbers for the United States. Reminders use local time. The iPhone app is portrait-only, targets iPhone rather than iPad, and requires iOS 18.0 or later. There is an Apple Watch companion.
