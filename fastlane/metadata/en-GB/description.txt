Cleaner 365 helps people review photos and videos, collect items for deletion, and keep private photo copies in a vault. It also offers password and email security tools.

• Review photos by month in rounds of 20 with “To bin”, “Keep” and “Undo swipe”.
• Try “Practice” with three sample cards without accessing Photos.
• Explore “Exact duplicates”, “Screenshots”, “Screen recordings” and “Large videos”.
• Use “Review bin” to preview, keep or confirm deletion of queued items.
• Keep verified copies of photos and Live Photos in the Face ID-protected “Vault” and restore copies to Photos.
• Use “Password generator”, “Email aliases”, “Email breach check” and “Password breach check”.
• Follow progress with “Monthly review” reminders, the “Season progress” widget and the Apple Watch companion.

Nothing leaves Photos until you confirm deletion.
